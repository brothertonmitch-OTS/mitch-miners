HOW TO PLAY

🕹

1. Auto-Aim & Dodge

All weapons, turrets, drones & pets fire automatically! Watch for pulsing red ✕ spawn markers and press SPACE / A (or tap DASH) to dodge through danger.

⚡

2. Ultimates & Pet Rally

Eliminating foes charges your unique Alien Ultimate — press E / X when it chimes at 100%! Press F / B to Rally Pets onto Bosses & Elites.

🔧

3. Merge, Lock & Synergies

Buy matching weapons (or tap ⚡ MERGE ALL) to upgrade up to Mk.IV★ Overcharged! Tap 🔒 LOCK to save cards for next wave, and click any Synergy in the Workshop to highlight matching gear.

📡

4. Live Arena Events

On Wave 2+, follow edge radar arrows for 📡 Overclock Beacons, 💰 Golden Cargo-Bots, ⚡ Glowing Floor Hubs, and ☄️ Ore Meteors for huge Scrap, Crates & Overdrive buffs!
